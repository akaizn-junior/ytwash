Save your place. Keep watching.

Open a video’s menu and choose Save, marked with a red lightning bolt. YTWash adds it to Watch Later and remembers the current time. Open it again in this browser to continue where you stopped.

When creator grouping is on, the playlist, watch-page sidebar, Play all, and Next follow the same creator order. Creators with one loaded video appear under Everything else.
